More than a viewfinder

Make room for the moment.

Framewise gives you just enough guidance to shape a photograph, then gets out of the way. Zoom stops adapt to your iPhone: Apple lists 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4× and 8× optical-quality options for the iPhone 18 Pro, while the standard iPhone 17 offers 0.5×, 1× and 2×. Local scanning works without an account; OpenRouter is optional and uses your own key.

01 / Scan

Choose your subject

Analyze one temporary still on device, or enable OpenRouter with your own key for a free vision-model scan. Tap a subject to retarget.

02 / Compose

Center the target

The dashed frame target stays fixed as the gyroscope-guided subject reticle moves over the live scene. Follow the move cue, hold near center to lock, then let the camera ease to the ideal zoom.

03 / Keep

Make it yours

Capture at the camera’s largest supported dimensions. Keep the full-size original, add a film look, or enable RAW for a DNG you can edit later.

Install your way.

Framewise is a standard iOS app package. Use the installer you already trust. The same IPA works with SideStore, AltStore Classic, Sideloadly, Feather, and other compatible IPA installers. LiveContainer is optional.
